# Logan Masters

My favorite city to visit is Green Mountain Falls, CO. I grew up there for a time, and my Nana still lives there. 
It's a very small town, it doesn't have a gas station or a grocery store, just houses, a school, and a few businesses.
It's up in the mountains of Colorado, near Pikes Peak, underneath the titular "Green Mountain" waterfalls.
